# Privacy Policy

We collect the minimal personal data required to provide the service:

- Email (for OTP-based login) and subscription status
- App usage metrics to improve reliability and performance

Data handling:

- OTP codes expire quickly and are not stored long-term
- Passwords are never stored; email OTP is used for authentication
- Payment references may be logged to match Wallet transfers (no card data)
- Data is stored securely; access is restricted to authorized admin

You may request deletion of your account by contacting `chaudreyadnan@gmail.com`.
